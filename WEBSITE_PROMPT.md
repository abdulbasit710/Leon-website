# Website build prompt

Build a responsive, production-quality React author website for **Leon Parrish** using the supplied approved design image as the primary visual reference. Match its composition, section order, visual rhythm, typography hierarchy, colors, and vintage memoir atmosphere as closely as possible. Use semantic JSX, accessible interactions, and clean CSS. The result must work on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Visual direction

- Near-black and deep olive backgrounds; antique gold accents; warm cream text; thin gold rules and borders.
- Editorial serif display type with large uppercase headlines, elegant italic secondary lines, and restrained sans-serif labels/body text.
- Tactile paper, aged photographs, handwritten-signature accents, tilted print ephemera, book-cover mockups, subtle grain, and a gold torn-paper divider.
- Spacious, premium editorial layout. Preserve the supplied logo if its original asset is available; otherwise make a temporary typographic mark clearly identified as a placeholder.

## Page sections, in order

1. Narrow top announcement strip and centered author logo/navigation.
2. Hero: “A LITTLE GRIT. A lot of story.”, introductory copy, two calls to action, layered family imagery, old paper/book elements, 1946 stamp.
3. Gold divider and small story navigation line.
4. About the author: image collage, “THE MAN BEHIND the memoir.”, short biography and three-date timeline.
5. Books: “A LIFETIME, on your shelf.”, two realistic book editions on a shelf, book and enquiry links.
6. Featured memoir: large tilted book visual, “THE BOY. THE JOURNEY. The Gaffer Above.”, synopsis and CTA.
7. FAQ accordion with five questions.
8. Reader response cards under “THE NEXT CHAPTER? Your words.”
9. Contact invitation and four-column footer.

## Behavior and quality

- All navigation must scroll to real sections. Mobile navigation must open and close with an accessible button. FAQ items must expand and collapse by keyboard and pointer.
- Use real approved photos, logo, cover art, exact book details, and confirmed contact information when supplied. Until then, use clearly replaceable original placeholders. Never invent a purchasing URL or claim a fictional image depicts Leon.
- Keep text contrast strong, images descriptive to screen readers, and buttons easy to tap.
- Deliver working source files and a local preview. Inspect the result at desktop and mobile widths and fix visible clipping, spacing, and hierarchy issues.

## Asset prompt used for the original website photo

Create a warm, candid vertical editorial photograph of a friendly older couple in a modest English home, gently smiling over an old paperback. Subtle family-photo nostalgia, muted olive and sepia grade, natural window light, authentic documentary feel, tactile film grain. Center the subjects and leave breathing room. No lettering, logos, graphics, or watermark. This is an original fictional scene inspired by the supplied design’s mood, not an exact depiction of a real person.
